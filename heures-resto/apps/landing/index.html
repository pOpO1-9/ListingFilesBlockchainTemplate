FAQ

Is the tax calculation official?

No. It’s an estimate to help you ballpark take-home pay. Always check your payslip / accountant.

Where is my data stored?

Hours are saved in your browser (localStorage). We only need your email for the license delivery via Stripe.

Chrome or iPhone?

Both. Install from Chrome’s menu, or Add to Home Screen on iPhone Safari.
